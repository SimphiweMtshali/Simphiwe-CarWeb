Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CarHouse may collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you browse the site, contact us, submit a vehicle listing, or request dealership registration.

1. Personal Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use CarHouse, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, city or location, vehicle information, dealership details, account login details, and any message or inquiry you choose to send to us.

2. How We Collect Information

We may collect information directly from you when you complete forms, contact us by email, submit a vehicle for sale, request dealership registration, or use features that store account-related details. We may also collect limited technical information such as browser type, device information, and usage data needed to improve site performance and security.

3. Why We Use Your Information

CarHouse may use personal information to respond to inquiries, publish and manage vehicle listings, review dealership applications, support user accounts, improve the website, detect misuse, and communicate important service-related updates. We aim to use information only for specific, legitimate, and relevant purposes.

4. Cookies and Usage Data

CarHouse may use cookies or similar technologies to understand page visits, improve performance, remember user preferences, and support future account features. Where cookies are used, they should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for site operation, analytics, and user experience.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell personal information. CarHouse may share information only where necessary to operate the platform, respond to your request, work with trusted service providers, comply with legal obligations, or protect the rights, safety, and integrity of the platform and its users.

6. Security Safeguards

CarHouse should take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against loss, unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or unlawful disclosure. These safeguards may include access controls, secure storage, limited staff access, and ongoing monitoring of sensitive workflows.

7. Retention of Information

Personal information should be kept only for as long as it is necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, to meet legal or record-keeping obligations, or to resolve disputes and maintain platform integrity. When information is no longer required, it should be securely deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, ask that incorrect information be corrected, request deletion where lawful, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. You may also ask how your information is being used and whether it has been shared.

9. Cross-Border Processing

If CarHouse later uses service providers or infrastructure located outside South Africa, personal information should be handled with appropriate safeguards and only where permitted by applicable privacy law.

10. Contact for Privacy Requests

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, please contact CarHouse at info@CarHouse.com. Additional contact details for the responsible information officer can be added here once formally designated.

11. Policy Updates

CarHouse may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in site features, operational needs, or applicable law. The latest version should always be published on this page with the most recent effective date.
